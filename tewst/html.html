მეგრული სამზარეულო

Megruli Samzareulo (მეგრული სამზარეულო) refers to the traditional cuisine of the Samegrelo (Megrelian) region in western Georgia, characterized by spicy and hearty dishes. Key features and dishes include: Ghomi: A staple dish made from coarse cornmeal, typically served with Sulguni cheese. Sulguni: A semi-hard, stretchy cheese often served in plates or smoked. Elarji: A dish made by mixing cornmeal (Ghomi) with Sulguni cheese. Megrelian Ajika: A spicy red pepper paste, often served with bread and butter. Other dishes: Kharcho (beef soup), Satsivi (walnut sauce with roasted meat), Kupati (intestines), and Mchadi (cornbread). The cuisine is noted for its use of fresh herbs, spices, and local wines, particularly the Ojaleshi grape variety.
