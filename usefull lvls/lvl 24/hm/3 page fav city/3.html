top 4 things to see in TOKYO

so in this page we going to talk about top 4 thing to see in tokyo

here we see Senso-ji Temple Tokyo's oldest temple, founded in 628 after two fishermen
reportedly pulled a golden statue of Kannon from the Sumida River. Destroyed and rebuilt many times over 1,400 years,
including by the 1945 firebombing; the current buildings date to 1958. Draws ~30 million visitors a year.

here we see Imperial Palace Built on the grounds of the former Edo Castle
(fortified from 1457, seat of the Tokugawa shoguns from 1603). After the Meiji Restoration, the emperor moved
here in 1868 when Edo was renamed Tokyo. Damaged in WWII, then rebuilt; the East Gardens remain free to explore.

Meiji Shrine Dedicated in 1920 to the deified spirits of Emperor Meiji and Empress Shoken, who led Japan's modernization.
The surrounding forest of ~120,000 trees was planted from donations across all of Japan. Destroyed in WWII and rebuilt in 1958

Tokyo Tower Opened in 1958 as Japan's primary broadcast tower, modeled on the Eiffel Tower at 333 m. Painted international orange and white for
aviation safety. Served as the city's main TV/radio antenna for over 50 years before being surpassed by Skytree in 2012, but remains an icon of post-war Tokyo.
