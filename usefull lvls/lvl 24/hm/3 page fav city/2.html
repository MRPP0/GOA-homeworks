TOKYO

Tokyo (東京都) is the capital and most populous city of Japan, with a population of about 14.25 million in the city proper and roughly 41 million in the Greater Tokyo Area — one of the world's largest metropolitan regions. Originally called Edo, it became Japan's political center in 1603 under the Tokugawa shogunate and was renamed "Eastern Capital" when the imperial capital moved there from Kyoto in 1868.
