who is sonic.exe

Sonic.EXE appears similar to Sonic the Hedgehog at first glance, with blue fur, white gloves, and red shoes. However, his appearance is twisted and unsettling. His eyes are completely black with glowing red pupils, and blood often streams from them. He usually wears an unnaturally wide grin filled with sharp teeth. His expression is calm, sinister, and predatory, giving the impression that he enjoys frightening or tormenting others. In the original fan story, Sonic.EXE is portrayed as a supernatural, malevolent entity that traps victims inside a haunted video game and toys with them before killing them.
