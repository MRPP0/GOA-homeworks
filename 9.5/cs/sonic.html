who is sonic?

Sonic the Hedgehog is a fictional blue hedgehog created by Sega. He is famous for his incredible speed and is the main hero of the Sonic video game series. Sonic spends his time protecting the world from the evil scientist Dr. Eggman (also known as Dr. Robotnik), who builds robots and tries to take over the world. Along the way, Sonic is helped by friends like Tails, a flying two-tailed fox, Knuckles, a strong echidna, and Amy Rose. Sonic first appeared in the 1991 game *Sonic the Hedgehog* and has since starred in many video games, TV shows, comics, and movies. He is known for his blue color, red shoes, confident attitude, and love of adventure. His signature ability is running at extremely high speeds, often said to be faster than the speed of sound.
